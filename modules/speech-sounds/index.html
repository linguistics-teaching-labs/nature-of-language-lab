Phonetics and phonology

Speech Sounds: Find the Pattern

Move from physical articulation to the sound patterns speakers know.

1 Decode a sound2 Test a contrast3 Infer a rule4 Conclude
Learning objectives
  • Describe consonants by voicing, place, and manner.
  • Distinguish contrast from predictable variation.
  • Infer a phonotactic pattern from data.
  • Separate sound-system differences from language rankings.
Step 1

Decode an articulatory clue

Choose a sound, then identify how it is produced.

Reading the symbols
