Meaning in Context: Said or Implied?

Separate what follows from the words from what a speaker suggests in context.

Learning objectives
  • Distinguish entailment, presupposition, and implicature.
  • Use contradiction and cancellation diagnostics.
  • Identify context-dependent communicative functions.
  • Explain why pragmatic meaning is constrained, not arbitrary.
