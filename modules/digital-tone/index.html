Digital pragmatics

Digital Tone and Context Explorer

Treat digital cues as resources interpreted in context—not fixed codes.

1 Configure a message 2 Compare contexts 3 Design a test 4 Calibrate a claim
Learning objectives
  • Identify contextual resources in digital interaction.
  • Compare plausible interpretations of identical wording.
  • Match a context claim to an appropriate research design.
  • Form a conclusion proportional to the evidence.
