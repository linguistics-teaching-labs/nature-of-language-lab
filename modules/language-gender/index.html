Form, Function, and Gender Stereotypes

Analyze what a feature does before assigning it to a type of speaker.

1 Explore function2 Change context3 Detect ideology4 Test perception
Learning objectives
  • Reject a strict women-men speech dichotomy.
  • Separate linguistic form from contextual function.
  • Identify gender ideologies in language evaluation.
  • Design evidence for listener-perception effects.
