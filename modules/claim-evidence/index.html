Critical reasoning

Claim and Evidence Inspector

Build an argument whose conclusion is no stronger than its evidence.

1 Unpack the claim2 Define the test3 Match evidence4 Stress-test5 Calibrate
Learning objectives
  • Identify scope, outcome, and causal language in a claim.
  • Operationalize a claim with measures and comparisons.
  • Distinguish relevant evidence from illustration or opinion.
  • Consider alternatives before reaching a conclusion.
Step 1

Unpack the claim

Select a course claim and identify what it asks the evidence to carry.

Step 2

Define a fair test

Choose one outcome, one comparison, and coverage proportionate to the claim.

Step 3

Match claim and evidence

Select every item needed to evaluate the active claim—and only those items.

Step 4

Stress-test alternatives

Check every explanation a strong design would need to address.

Step 5

Calibrate the conclusion

Choose the conclusion whose strength matches the possible evidence and remaining limits.

Reusable inspection routine

Scope → measure → comparison → alternatives → conclusion

Apply the sequence whenever a linguistic claim moves from observation to explanation or evaluation.
