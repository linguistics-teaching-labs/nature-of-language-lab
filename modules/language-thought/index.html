Language and Thought: How Strong Is the Claim?

Reject determinism without flattening evidence of subtler relationships.

1 Read a finding2 Calibrate3 Stress-test4 Plan evidence
Learning objectives
  • Distinguish association, influence, and determination.
  • Match a claim’s strength to its evidence.
  • Identify task, cultural, and sampling alternatives.
  • Preserve nuance in cross-linguistic comparison.
